Lesson 1

Introduction to Affiliate Marketing

Welcome to Lesson 1 of Zero to Affiliate Income. In this lesson, you will learn what affiliate marketing is, how it works, and how people can earn commissions by promoting products or services belonging to other businesses.

What is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a business model where you promote a product or service using a unique affiliate link. When someone makes a qualifying purchase through your link, you may receive a commission.

How Does It Work?

The basic process is simple:

Choose a product → Get your affiliate link → Promote the product → Someone purchases through your link → You receive a commission.

Important Things to Remember

Affiliate marketing is not a guaranteed way to make money. Your results depend on factors such as the product you promote, your audience, your marketing strategy, and your consistency.

Lesson Summary

Affiliate marketing allows you to promote products or services and potentially earn commissions from qualifying sales. In the next lesson, we will look at how to choose products that are suitable for promotion.
